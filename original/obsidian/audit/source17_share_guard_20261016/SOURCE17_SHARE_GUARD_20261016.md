## Checked numbers

The article's printed p. 65 describes **76.5% recovered waste heat + 21% heat-pump electricity + 2.5% gas**, hence **97.5% heat generated by heat pumps** and 100% total generation. The 97.5% figure must NOT be presented as 97.5% *direct waste heat*: it includes heat-pump electrical input.

If all these percentages share the same annual-energy denominator, the implied ratio of heat-pump output to electricity is `(76.5 + 21) / 21 = 4.642857`. This exceeds the article's Table 3 reported COP interval `3.76..3.9`. The narrative additionally gives `3.67` as its lower bound after mentioning `3.76`. These are **unresolved accounting / reporting discrepancies**, not proof of a physical-law violation or proof the authors are wrong: the source may use different system boundaries, rounding, seasonal definitions, or a transcription error. Do not calibrate the DCHR model to `97.5%` until the denominator and heat-pump electric accounting are reconciled against the original tables/figures and equations.
