# Source catalog — исходные 31 работы

Исходный документ: **Datacentra_teplo_vedecke_prace.pdf**, составлен 22.09.2026. Ниже каталог для навигации по проекту; полные DOI/библиографию сверять с исходным PDF перед формальной публикацией.

## Финляндия — Espoo / Helsinki

1. Wahlroos, Pärssinen, Manner, Syri (2017) — *Utilizing data center waste heat in district heating – Impacts on energy efficiency and prospects for low-temperature district heating networks*. **Core model / annual dispatch.**
2. Wahlroos, Pärssinen, Rinne, Syri, Manner (2018) — *Future views on waste heat utilization – Case of data centers in Northern Europe*. **Barriers / heat quality / economics.**
3. Pärssinen, Wahlroos, Manner, Syri (2019) — *Waste heat from data centers: An investment analysis*. **Investment layer.**
4. Hiltunen, Syri (2020) — *Highly Renewable District Heat for Espoo Utilizing Waste Heat Sources*. **System scenarios / prices.**
5. Hiltunen, Syri (2021) — *Low-temperature waste heat enabling abandoning coal in Espoo district heating system*. **Liquid cooling / source temperature.**
6. Khosravi, Laukkanen, Vuorinen, Syri (2021) — *Waste heat recovery from a data centre and 5G smart poles for low-temperature district heating network*. **Low-temperature DH.**

## Швеция — Stockholm

7. Huang et al. (2020) — *A review of data centers as prosumers in district energy systems*. **Integration schemes / review.**
8. Tofani (2022) — *Integration of excess heat from Data Centres in Stockholm district heating system*. **Stakeholders / commercial barriers.**
9. Sintong (2023) — *Data Centres as Prosumers: A Techno-Economic Analysis*. **ODH contract / electricity prices.**
10. Lind, Rundgren (2017) — *Industrial Symbiosis in Heat Recovery Collaborations between Data Centers and District Heating and Cooling Companies*. **Ownership / willingness to invest.**

## Дания — Odense / national system

11. Petrović et al. (2020) — *The role of data centres in the future Danish energy system*. **Energy-system context.**
12. Sorknæs et al. (2022) — *The benefits of 4th generation district heating and energy efficient datacentres*. **Supply temperature / 4GDH.**
13. Jerez Monsalves, Bergaentzlé, Keles (2023) — *Impacts of flexible-cooling and waste-heat recovery from data centres on energy systems*. **Flexibility / control.**

## Великобритания — London / UK

14. Davies, Maidment, Tozer (2016) — *Using data centres for combined heating and cooling: An investigation for London*. **Classic techno-economic case.**
15. Davies et al. (2023) — *Evaluation of low temperature waste heat as a low carbon heat resource in the UK*. **National urban potential.**
16. Marques, Tozer, Revesz et al. (2020) — *GreenSCIES – Integration with Data Centres*. **5GDH / local energy system.**

## Германия / Испания / Италия / Болгария

17. Jürgens et al. (2024) — *Covering District Heating Demand with Waste Heat from Data Centres: A Feasibility Study in Frankfurt, Germany*. **Primary calibration target for existing buildings.**
18. Oró, Taddeo, Salom (2019) — *Waste heat recovery from urban air cooled data centres to increase energy efficiency of district heating networks*. **Air cooling / ERF / economic caution.**
19. Socci, Rocchetti, Verzino, Zini, Talluri (2025) — *Enhancing third-generation district heating networks with data centre waste heat recovery*. **Existing 3GDH.**
20. Verzino, Talluri, Rocchetti, Socci (2026) — *Smart Reuse of Waste Heat from Data Centres: Energy and Exergy Analysis for a District-Heating Network in Bulgaria*. **Varna / THUNDER / storage / exergy.**

## Обзорные работы

21. Ebrahimi, Jones, Fleischer (2014) — *Review of data center cooling technology, operating conditions and low-grade waste heat recovery opportunities*. **Cooling temperatures.**
22. Yuan et al. (2023) — *Waste heat recoveries in data centers: A review*. **Technology taxonomy.**
23. Yuan et al. (2025) — *Data center waste heat for district heating networks: A review*. **DH / economics / legislation / storage.**
24. He, Ding, Liu, Li (2018) — *Analysis of a district heating system using waste heat in a distributed cooling data center*. **Real DC + heat pump.**

## Официальные источники / политика / implementation

25. EU Directive 2023/1791 + Delegated Regulation EU 2024/1364. **Reporting / waste-heat assessment.**
26. European Commission DG CNECT (2024) — *Study on Optimising Synergies between Data Centres and Energy Systems (DCESS)*. **EU case studies / ERF target discussion.**
27. Euroheat & Power et al. — *From Data Centres to District Heating & Cooling*. **Industry / policy recommendations.**
28. EU Covenant of Mayors (2023) — *Heat recovery from data centres – Stockholm case study*. **Business model / connection repayment.**
29. Greater London Authority / AECOM (2025) — *Optimising Data Centres in London – Heat Reuse*. **City toolkit / ownership scenarios.**
30. Fjernvarme Fyn / Nguyen (2021) — *Utilizing Facebook Data Center Surplus Heat for District Heating in Odense*. **Operator case.**
31. ReUseHeat (Horizon 2020). **Demonstrations / business models / contracts.**
