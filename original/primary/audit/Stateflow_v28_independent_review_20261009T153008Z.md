## Mathematical and primary-evidence findings

No allocation/equation defect was found for the explicitly declared finite-domain, exponential-passive, loss-first policy. The adapter previews `E_after=E_start*exp(-lambda*dt)` when computing HP charging headroom; the chart selector and allocator use the same state. Charge is bounded by `(capacity-E_after)/(eta_charge*dt)`, discharge by `E_after*eta_discharge/dt`, and the allocator accounts for standing and conversion losses. Source heat, HP electricity and boiler heat close the overall thermal boundary. Backup is thermal output, and unmet demand is an accounting deficit. The separate MATLAB dispatch and fixed-COP Python oracle represent the same declared discrete policy through separate implementations.

Passive attenuation solves `E'=-lambda*E` exactly under constant coefficients and has the semigroup property. It remains nonnegative when `lambda*dt>1`, unlike the legacy linear map. A continuous inverse-hour rate differs from an hourly fractional loss: an observed fraction `f` implies `lambda=-log(1-f) / h`. Explicit conversion to the legacy per-step coefficient demonstrates numerical parity only. This does not provide equipment calibration.

The active update `E_next=E_start*exp(-lambda*dt)+P*dt` applies interval forcing after passive loss. For simultaneous continuous constant power, the correct unconstrained state is `E_start*exp(-lambda*dt)+P*(1-exp(-lambda*dt))/lambda`. The split therefore overestimates charging and underestimates discharging for the same positive step duration, with leading one-step discrepancy `P*lambda*dt^2/2`. The existing source/results documents correctly retain this limitation; timestep convergence of passive or unconstrained charging cases does not certify arbitrary saturation or switching trajectories.

This reviewer independently viewed the already acquired `audit/TES_Cadau_v28_page4.png`: Cadau et al. (2019), article/PDF p4 Eq. (4), contains ambient loss `-U*A*(T_i-T_amb)`. The same page explicitly defines a uniform temperature within each node. Local extracted p6 requires at least two nodes for the paper's routing implementation. Nomenclature on extracted p20 gives mass kg, specific heat J/(kg K), area m2, U W/(m2 K), and time seconds. These support the source audit's constant-C, no-flow, no-inter-node reduction; they do not identify the project's scalar model with that stratified implementation. This is an additional local equation inspection, not a new source acquisition, full article signoff or paper-result replication.

The useful-energy reference remains a material physical limitation. If operational energy is relative to a delivery temperature above ambient, its ambient-loss law is affine: `E_use'=-lambda*(E_use+E_offset)`. The current pure exponential energy state has neither that offset nor a temperature/cutoff event. The report appropriately labels it phenomenological zero-equilibrium/ambient-referenced energy. A temperature/cutoff-aware store with explicit saturation events is the next corrective physical-model task, after this implementation gate closes.
